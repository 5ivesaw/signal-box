02 / UNDER THE SURFACE

Wear comes from the process.

A catalog of mechanisms, with the method left visible.

01—

Signal & tape

Reduce chroma bandwidth. Delay color against luminance. Disturb line timing, lose RF recovery, and let a head-switch disturb the bottom of the picture.

02—

Film & exposure

Grain responds to luminance. Highlights bloom into neighboring pixels. Registration drifts, emulsion blots, and a frame burns in the projector gate.

03—

Time & memory

Weave fields from different moments. Hold, echo, reorder, or slice time across the frame. Moving previews reveal what a still cannot.

04—

Sound & generations

Irregular sample clocks create wow and flutter. Forward pre-echo anticipates a sound. Real encode/decode passes build compression artifacts.

03 / HONEST LABELS

Know what you’re applying.

Process · model · authored

Process uses the named operation. Model approximates physical or perceptual behavior. Authored mixes material you supply. Some advanced techniques require externally prepared footage or sound; the catalog says so.

A real browser port

The Python effect engine runs locally through Pyodide; FFmpeg runs through WebAssembly. No backend is involved. Runtime files load on demand and remain browser-cacheable. GSM, Codec2 and Rubber Band pitch/formant processing require the desktop engine. All other catalog entries have browser implementations; authored entries need supplied assets.

Choose the right scale

Start at 480 pixels. Expensive effects can be much slower than real time. Browser memory is finite: large source files, lengthy jobs and huge audio buffers may need the desktop version. Analysis examines the whole source in small chunks. Adaptive choices are explained heuristics, not a trained neural network.

Keep your project

Export your settings as JSON and reopen them in the browser or terminal app. Media files need to be reselected after a reload. Original files are never modified. No analytics, account, camera access, or hidden uploads.
